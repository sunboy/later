# Openings teardown: how apps make the premise personal in the first minute

Meaning, not craft (craft: `../onboarding-flows.md`).

- **Credits:** 1,241 Appllama credits before and 1,141 after, so 100 were spent.
- **Coverage:** 33 apps, through their screens and flow videos.
- **Screenshots:** scratchpad only, none in the repository.

## 1. Per-app findings

| App | Premise and speaker | Own data first appears | "For me" moment | Trust ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spark Mail AI | The system speaks, then the assistant ("I…") | Starter prompts built from real mail ("Show latest emails from FitOn") | Asked to archive, it shows a checklist of exact emails with Archive / Skip, then reports "I've archived 11…" | "Private by design… not used to train AI" |
| Rocket Money | Options in the user's voice | Life-fact cards ("I rent", "I have kids") | Sample alerts: "Your Spotify price increased by $4.99" | "We'll detect your subscriptions", plus a "bank-level security" chip |
| Copilot | "Let's Do Money Better", demo from screen 1 | Empty dashboard skeleton | Job shown before the ask | "Read-only… We don't sell your data", then "Continue without" |
| Superlist | "Which areas of your life…" | "Which sound like you?" (Parent, Planning a move) | "Creating your starting system" ends in sections you can remove with x | Legal only |
| Cozi | "Morris, welcome to Cozi" (name before brand) | The user's own event | "Hooray! Bike ride has been added… a small victory" | None; it ends on an empty agenda |
| Tiimo, Structured | Pills in the user's voice ("Remember to call mum back"); "Let's plan today" | The user's real tasks on a timeline | "That's how it starts"; "That's a great plan" | The notification preview uses the user's item ("Eat Lunch – in 5 min") |
| Fabulous | "Not magic. Just science." | Every answer acknowledged ("Got it!") | "Morris's Contract", hold to commit | The fairy asks: "I'll remind you…" / "No, I'll do this on my own" |
| Rosebud | "Hi Morris! What brings you…" | The first act is the product: "Highlight of your day?" | The reflection threads in earlier answers | "An AI thinking partner… not human" |
| Purpose | A bird speaks in the first person throughout | "What should I call you? This helps me personalise" | "Here's what you told me", then "Morris, Here's How I'll Help" | Notifications called check-ins |
| Noom | The mascot speaks, with "a few quick questions" | The user's reunion becomes a dated milestone | "…by the reunion" | Every question gives its reason |
| Hinge | "Designed to be deleted" | A "You're 25" confirm card | A live profile card showing how answers will appear | One consequence line per ask |
| Speak, Praktika | Tutor chat is the onboarding | Follow-ups quote answers | User does the core action | "I'll remind you… change this later" |
| CARROT | "Hello, human! I, CARROT…" | The user picks the personality | Voice established within four screens | "First week's on me" |
| Tolan | The Oracle speaks in subtitles | Each answer interpreted ("…shows guts") | Naming the character | "…like that new song they found for you" |
| Replika | "The AI to do life with" | Chips in the user's voice, never reflected back | "How should Sun talk?" previews the voice | "Keep up with your plans… disconnect anytime" |
| Wabi | "Gets your life organised" (Later's own premise) | Never | None: "what can i help you with today?" over a blank composer | Buried in settings |
| Gemini, Meta AI, ChatGPT, Claude, Comet | Third-person claims | A name at most | None; blank composer | Disclaimers outrank the promise |

Also studied: Duolingo, Finch, Napper, BePresent, Inbox Zapper (paywall before the result; its "we never read the content" is contradicted by the actual scope), Kindroid, Nomi, Character.AI, TimeTree, Dear Me, Me+, Meditopia, Fluently, Yazio and Hearing Buddy. No consumer app in the library connects mail and calendar to an agent during onboarding, so Later's trust ask has to be built from adjacent patterns.

## 2. Structural moves

| # | Move | Apps | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The character speaks first and names itself | Duolingo, Speak, Praktika, CARROT, Purpose, Tolan | Screens 1–2 |
| 2 | The cost is stated up front ("7 quick questions") | Duolingo, Praktika, Noom | Screens 2–3 |
| 3 | Questions as conversation: each gives its reason, and each answer is acknowledged | Fabulous, Noom, Tolan, Napper, Speak | Every question, 5–12 screens |
| 4 | The user's facts become an artefact (milestone, sections, profile card) | Noom, Superlist, Structured, Rosebud, Hinge, BePresent | Within 6–15 screens |
| 5 | "Here's what you told me", an editable summary | Purpose, Napper, Superlist, Fabulous | One screen, before commitment |
| 6 | The user does the core action once, on their own item | Spark AI, Cozi, Tiimo, Rosebud, Praktika | One screen, the climax |
| 7 | Trust ask = need + consequence + refusal + undo, with a preview of the output | Copilot, Hinge, Replika, Tolan, Structured | One per ask, after value is shown |

## 3. Voice and interaction

**Voice**

- The user's voice for choices ("I rent").
- The character's "I" for promises ("I'll remind you").
- The company's "we" only for privacy.
- The user's name returned in the character's mouth.
- An acknowledgement before every next question.
- Concrete nouns from the user's world ("Doordash renews in 5 days"), never category nouns.
- Permissions named for the relationship ("so your Tolan can reach you").

**Interactions that carry meaning**

- Hold to commit (Fabulous, Praktika).
- Remove with x from a generated set, the agent contract in miniature (Superlist, Spark).
- "Does this sound like you?" mirror cards.
- A tone picker that previews the voice (CARROT, Replika).
- A narrated build: the wait becomes evidence.

## 4. "This does things for you"

Only **Spark's assistant** succeeds: verbs named at consent, starters from real mail, a checklist of exact items awaiting a tap, then a first-person report naming senders. Rosebud and Purpose succeed narrowly by playing the user's words back.

**Wabi** (Later's exact promise), **Gemini**, **Meta AI**, **ChatGPT**, **Claude** and **Comet** all fail the same way. They end on "What can I help with?" over an empty composer, handing the job back to the user. That is the opposite of an agent.

## 5. Later's current front door (walked headless at 390×844)

**The flow, in four parts:**

1. **Story scenes:** the title, then "Life sends you a lot", "Keep and the crew sort it", "Only what needs you", "Not now? Flick it down" and "Nothing leaves without your yes".
2. **Set-up:** "Yours. In your own account.", a computer with a pairing code, the Gmail app password, the iCal private link, then "School apps too?".
3. **The read:** "Keep is reading", showing "$0.07 of a $1.00 cap".
4. **The result:** "Keep found 4 Spaces" (House move, School, A birthday, Taxes as icon cards), "Your first deck" (a blank envelope card), then "Open Today".

| Pattern | Later today |
|---|---|
| Character speaks | **No.** Keep never says a word. The headlines are captions about Keep in the third person, and the crew (Wren, Scout, Juno) appear in subtitles before anyone has met them. |
| Name, acknowledgement, questions | **None.** Nothing about the user shapes anything before the read, although "moving? kids?" would seed Spaces. |
| Own data | It first appears at step 13, as icon labels with no evidence: no emails and no dates. The "needs you" and deck cards are blank. |
| Core action on own item | **Never.** The yes is practised on an empty card ("Only practice. Nothing was sent."). |
| Trust asks | "Wren reads it. Nothing is sent without your yes." is good. "Gmail app password" and "secret address in iCal format" read like a mechanical permissions screen. No ask previews its output. |
| Cost stated | Spend is shown, which is excellent and unique. Time is not: the move to a computer arrives unannounced at step 8. |

**Verdict:** every beat teaches a gesture; none shows Keep understanding *this* person. The mechanics are right; the voice and the user's nouns are missing.

## 6. Three structures

### A. "Tell Keep about your life" (question-led: Superlist, Noom, Purpose)

1. **Tap the title.** Keep says: "Hi, I'm Keep. I hold what you'd rather not carry."
2. **Type your name.** "Nice to meet you, Sam. Five quick questions, then I'll get to work."
3. **Tap the life facts that are true** ("Moving house", "Kids at school"). Each is acknowledged, and a Space card fans into Keep's hand.
4. **Pick what weighs most.** Keep names the crew member who will take it: "Scout keeps the calendar."
5. **Pick Keep's voice** (Brief, Warm or Dry). Keep re-says the last line in that voice.
6. **Review "Here's what you told me".** Remove anything with x.
7. **The mail ask:** need, "never send without your yes", and undo. It previews the card a mail item will become.
8. **Watch a narrated read, with the cap showing.** Real finds tick into the Spaces ("Removals quote from Pickfords").
9. **Hold yes on a real draft, or send it to later.** "That was your first yes." Today opens.

### B. "Show me with my own mail" (agent-led: Spark AI, Copilot)

1. **A live sample deck.** Keep speaks: "This is a morning with me."
2. **Flick a sample card to later.** "It comes back Thursday."
3. **"Do it with mine".** The cost is stated: "Two connections, three minutes, capped at $1."
4. **The mail and calendar asks.** Each gives a list of verbs: reads, sorts, drafts; never sends or deletes.
5. **The read, narrated with real senders.** "214 emails, newest first."
6. **"I think these are your Spaces".** Each comes with evidence ("House move: 6 emails, completion 12 Dec"). Rename or remove any of them.
7. **One proposed action.** A checklist of exact items, each with Approve or Skip.
8. **Hold yes.** "Done: I filed 9 receipts into Taxes."
9. **Choose when the deck arrives.** Today opens with the user's own first deck.

### C. "Meet the crew" (character-led: Finch, Praktika, Tolan)

1. **Tap to wake Keep.** "Oh! Hello. I'm Keep. Who are you?"
2. **Say or type your name.** Keep repeats it and fans out empty cards: "Let's fill these."
3. **Pick the heavy parts of life.** A crew card is dealt for each, and each introduces itself ("I'm Ledger. Receipts are my thing.").
4. **Rename or recolour a crew member** (optional). Ownership.
5. **Each crew member makes its own ask** ("Let me read your mail. I'll never send without you."), with a preview of what it hands back.
6. **The crew goes off to read.** Each orbits Keep, with per-member progress under the cap.
7. **The crew returns one by one,** each with a found card ("Juno: Year 4 trip form, due Friday"). Keep or flick each.
8. **Send one find to later and hold yes on another.** Gestures learned on real items.
9. **Keep signs off.** "I'll bring you three things each morning." Today opens.

### Choosing between them

- **A** has the lowest risk and seeds Spaces before the read.
- **B** proves "does things for you" most strongly, but it needs a fast read.
- **C** has the most character and reuses the crew art.

All three move the computer set-up later, or announce it as a cost before it arrives.
