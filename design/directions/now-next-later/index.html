Now, Next, Later

The one idea. The app is a horizon. Day at the top, night at the bottom. You set a thing down and it sinks into Later; it rises again at the time you chose. “Later” is the verb the whole app uses.

Type. Archivo, on its width axis. Now is set wide and heavy, Next at normal width, Later narrow and light, so distance in time is drawn as width; the wordmark recedes letter by letter. Instrument Sans carries everything you read.

Spaces apart. Colour says when, never which. A Space is told by its glyph and its shape: square for a project, round for an area, stacked for a list.

Daybreak #FF5B24Whatever needs you. If it is orange, it is waiting on your decision.
Midnight #1B2550Whatever the agents hold: work under way, things resting, and the button that hands a job over. Ink and paper are tints of the two.
Moonlight #D7F64AOnly the moment something changes hands: it lands in Later, comes back on time, or gets done. Never a resting state.

What is risky

A wall of orange at the top can read as alarm on a heavy day, so the Now band needs a hard cap. Forcing every Space into one of three bands hides mixed states, such as a Space that is both waiting and urgent. Width-axis type gets fiddly at small sizes, and the dusk band is the weakest colour field of the three.
