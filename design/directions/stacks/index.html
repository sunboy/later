Desk Today Templates

Stacks

Every Space is a small deck on your desk. Its thickness is how much it needs you, and its top card carries the button.

You act from the overview without opening anything: tap the button, or drag the card off and the next one rises. Spaces with nothing for you turn face down and show what the agent is doing.

Three colours, three jobs

UltramarineWhat the agents hold. Card backs, index corners, the Today desk, navigation.
RoseWhat waits on you. Every card edge under a top card is rose, so thickness reads as need.
LimeThe one thing to do now. One button per screen, and the cards you have cleared today.

Spaces are told apart by the back of their cards: twelve patterns and glyphs in ultramarine, no extra hues.

Type

Funnel Display

Chunky and slightly squared, so a short ask on a card reads like a label printed on it. Funnel Sans, its sibling, carries detail and meta.

What is risky

Thickness is a blunt signal: four small cards look heavier than one urgent one. The overview is busy with nine buttons, and drag-to-defer must never feel like deleting.
