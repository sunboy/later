Templates Setups are people you can follow.

Live.

Your Spaces behave like people you follow. A ring says one has something for you; a tap plays it as a short story you can decide in; the feed shows each agent at work, with the one decision on the tile.

What is risky

Social mechanics train fast, thumb-first behaviour, and that is the wrong reflex for a payment or a sent email. The design leans on two guards: anything irreversible names its exact arguments on the button, and double-tap only queues or approves things that can be undone until a stated time. Rings can also start to feel like obligations. They have to vanish when a Space has nothing, and the feed has to stop.

North stars

A segmented story viewer from a large social app, for the progress bar and the thumb-height action row. A self-care pet app’s home, for one deep field colour covering real area with a warm signal colour kept for progress, and tactile buttons on the card itself. A creator profile, for the stats, the paired follow and message buttons and the highlight rings, which become what this setup does.

Three colours, three jobs

Cobalt #2234F0
Structure and action. The stage band at the top of every screen, the rings, the story backdrop and every primary button.
Blush #FFCFD8
Warmth and the human layer. The paper everything sits on, half the Space fields, and the places that are about you.
Tangerine #FF6B2C
The moment that matters. Waiting counts, the next card in your day, the swipe-up act and the clear. Never decoration.

Telling Spaces apart

No twelve hues. Each Space is a glyph on one of two bases, cobalt or blush, with one of six patterns: dots, stripes, grid, checks, waves or rays. Twelve identities from the same two colours.

A display face with chunky, slightly squared curves for the numbers and names that carry a decision (£1,240, Sat 10:30), and its text sibling for everything you read. Heavy weights only where the money or the date is.
