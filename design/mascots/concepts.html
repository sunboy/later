Later mascot concepts

One chief replaces the plus button and is the way in to talk, type and capture. Its crew are the agents working in your Spaces. Three families, each drawn from a few vector parts and shown in every state: large, at 24 px, as a bare silhouette, in the tab bar, and dealing a crew member to a Space and taking it back. Tap any state to hold it in the large view.

The Deck wins, because it is made of the app and moves like it.

Keep is a fanned hand of three cards with a dog-eared top card. Its states are things cards do: the fan breathes, the dog-ear lifts to listen, the cards riffle to think, square up into a stack to work, deal a clay card up when you are needed, snap wide open when work lands, and sink behind the horizon to wait for later. The silhouette row proves it: with every colour and both eyes removed, each state is still a different shape. Putting a crew member on a Space is dealing one card from the hand, and Keep’s fan is one card thinner until it comes back.

Working names. The chief is Keep; the crew are Wren (index tab), Scout (clipped corner), Juno (arched top) and Ledger (receipt edge). Clay appears only for waiting on you and error, citrus only for done. Weakest of the three is Hands: it reads well small but is an indicator, not a family. What survives from it: done should read as a tick at a glance, which the Deck gets from its wide citrus fan and closed slots.
