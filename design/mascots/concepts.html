Later mascot concepts

One chief replaces the plus button and is the way in to talk, type and capture. Its crew are the agents working in your Spaces. Three families, each built from simple vector parts, shown in every state, at 24 px and large, on light and dark. Not aliens, not animals or people, not plain geometric figures.

The Deck wins, because it is made of the app.

The chief is a stack of cards and each crew member is one card dealt from it. Putting someone on a Space is dealing a card into that Space, and the chief’s stack gets thinner while they are out. Nothing about it needs explaining, and nothing about it belongs to another product. The eyes are slots cut through the card, so the colour behind them carries meaning: clay rises behind the chief only when something needs you, citrus shows only when something lands.

Working names. The chief is Keep; the crew are Wren (tab), Scout (clipped corner), Juno (arch) and Ledger (receipt edge). The floats lost because the sea is a metaphor the palette suggested, not one the product owns. The hands lost because they are a status indicator, not a family. What survives from Hands: the done state should read as a tick at a glance, which the Deck gets from its closed, smiling slots and the citrus card beneath.
